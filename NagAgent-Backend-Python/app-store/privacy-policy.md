# Privacy Policy for NagAgent

**Last Updated**: January 2025  
**Effective Date**: January 2025

## Introduction

NagAgent Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") operates the NagAgent mobile application and website (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Service.

Your privacy is important to us. This policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information**: Email address, username, password (encrypted)
- **Profile Data**: Selected AI personality, motivational preferences, timezone
- **Goal and Task Data**: Daily goals, tasks, completion status, notes
- **User-Generated Content**: Excuse text, custom goal descriptions, feedback

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data**: App interactions, feature usage, session duration, screen views
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, app version, unique device identifiers
- **Performance Data**: Crash reports, error logs, performance metrics (anonymized)

### Information From Third Parties
- **Push Notification Tokens**: From Apple Push Notification Service for iOS notifications
- **Payment Information**: From Apple App Store for subscription management (we don't store payment details)
- **Analytics Data**: From Apple App Store Connect for app performance metrics

## How We Use Your Information

### Core Functionality
- **Personalized AI Responses**: Generate customized roasts and motivation based on your personality selection and behavior patterns
- **Task Management**: Track your goals, tasks, and progress over time
- **Notifications**: Send relevant push notifications based on your preferences and activity
- **Progress Reports**: Create daily, weekly, and monthly accountability reports

### Service Improvement
- **AI Enhancement**: Improve our AI models' accuracy and personality responses (using anonymized data)
- **Feature Development**: Analyze usage patterns to develop new features and improvements
- **Bug Fixes**: Identify and resolve technical issues and crashes
- **Performance Optimization**: Optimize app performance and user experience

### Communication
- **Customer Support**: Respond to your inquiries, technical issues, and feedback
- **Service Updates**: Notify you about important changes to the Service
- **Marketing Communications**: Send promotional content (only with your consent)

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do Not Sell Your Data
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

### Service Providers
We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our Service:

- **Cloud Hosting**: Railway.app or similar for backend infrastructure
- **AI Services**: OpenAI for natural language processing (data is not stored by OpenAI)
- **Analytics**: Firebase Analytics for usage analytics (anonymized data)
- **Customer Support**: Support platforms for handling user inquiries
- **Payment Processing**: Apple App Store for subscription management

All service providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information when required by law or to protect our rights, including:
- Responding to legal process (subpoenas, court orders)
- Enforcing our Terms of Service
- Protecting against fraud or security threats
- Protecting the safety of our users or the public

## Data Security

### Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption**: All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+ and at rest using AES-256
- **Access Controls**: Limited employee access with multi-factor authentication required
- **Regular Security Audits**: Periodic security assessments and penetration testing
- **Secure Infrastructure**: Hosted on secure cloud platforms with industry-standard protections

### Organizational Safeguards
- **Employee Training**: All employees receive privacy and security training
- **Data Access Policies**: Strict policies governing who can access user data and for what purpose
- **Incident Response Plan**: Procedures for responding to potential data breaches

## Your Rights and Choices

### Account Control
- **Access**: You can access your account information through the app settings
- **Update**: You can modify your profile, preferences, and goal data at any time
- **Delete**: You can delete specific tasks, goals, or your entire account

### Communication Preferences
- **Push Notifications**: Control notification types and frequency in app settings
- **Marketing Communications**: Opt out of promotional emails at any time

### Data Rights (GDPR/CCPA)
If you're in the EU, UK, or California, you have additional rights:
- **Right to Know**: Request information about what personal data we collect and how we use it
- **Right to Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Right to Rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
- **Right to Erasure**: Request deletion of your personal data
- **Right to Portability**: Request your data in a portable format
- **Right to Restrict Processing**: Request that we limit how we process your data
- **Right to Object**: Object to certain types of processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@nagagent.ai.

## Data Retention

### Account Data
- Active accounts: Data retained while your account is active
- Deleted accounts: Most data deleted within 30 days, some anonymized data may be retained for analytics

### Usage Analytics
- Anonymized usage data may be retained indefinitely for service improvement
- Personal identifiers are removed from analytics data

### Legal Requirements
- Some data may be retained longer if required by law or for legitimate business purposes (fraud prevention, dispute resolution)
